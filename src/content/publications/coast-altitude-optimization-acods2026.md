abstract: "Formal write-up of the altitude-coverage-accuracy coupling in shared-camera aerial-ground cooperative tracking. We formalize altitude as a coupled perception variable and derive a closed-form altitude scheduling policy for UAV-UGV cooperative tracking. The framework incorporates a belief-space Model Predictive Control (MPC) formulation and an event-triggered aerial planner that achieves a 25× compute reduction while preserving state estimation quality. The policy and theoretical derivations are validated across a complete, reproducible ROS 2/Gazebo testbed across variable terrain curvatures and noise regimes."
